About This Platform

A smart job hiring system that connects job seekers with the right opportunities and helps companies find the best talent — efficiently.

What is this system?

This is a web-based Job Hiring System built to simplify the recruitment process for both companies and job seekers. Companies can post job openings, manage applications, and find the most suitable candidates — all from one dashboard.

Job seekers can browse available jobs, apply with their CV, and track their application status in real time. The system uses AI-based skill matching to recommend the most relevant jobs based on the applicant's CV.

💼

Post & Manage Jobs

Companies post jobs and manage all incoming applications from a single dashboard.

How It Works

01
Create an Account

Register as a Job Seeker or a Company. Each role has its own dedicated dashboard and features.

02
Post or Browse Jobs

Companies post jobs with required skills and details. Job seekers browse and search available listings.

03
Apply & Get Matched

Job seekers apply by uploading their CV. The system calculates a match score based on required skills.

04
Review Applications

Companies review all applicants, view their CVs, check match scores, and filter top candidates.

05
Accept or Reject

Companies accept or reject applicants directly from their dashboard with a single click.

06
Track Your Status

Job seekers can monitor all their applications and see whether they are pending, accepted, or rejected.
